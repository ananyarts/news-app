The Daily Brief
===============

The Daily Brief is a Django news application. Readers follow articles from
publishers and independent journalists. Journalists write articles and
newsletters. Editors review and approve articles; when an article is
approved it is emailed to subscribers and logged through the project's own
REST endpoint, ``/api/approved/``.
